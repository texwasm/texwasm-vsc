After a successful build, the PDF opens automatically beside your LaTeX source. You can also reopen it at any time with the **View PDF** button above.

The PDF is written next to your `.tex` file (or into the folder set with `texwasm.outputDirectory`). Recompile after making changes to refresh it.

To jump from a line in your source to the matching page in the PDF, use **Forward Search** (`Ctrl+Alt+J`, or `Cmd+Alt+J` on macOS).
